# Prototype B2 Fire and Water VFX

Open `Assets/Scenes/TemplateB2.unity` and enter Play Mode. Choose **Tools → FOCUS → B2 → Preview Fire and Water VFX** to see the full sequence.

Assemble `WATER` during a fire. The dinosaur turns toward the flame and spits the water ball from its mouth.

The package loads only in `TemplateB2` at runtime. It uses the scene's existing fire scheduler, dinosaur, and typewriter. The three `strict/grid` textures are from PVFX Foundry 1.0 and covered by the bundled license. The steam, droplet trail, and hiss are generated at runtime.
